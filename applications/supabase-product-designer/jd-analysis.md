# Supabase — Product Designer

Source: pasted by candidate 2026-08-28 (full text in `jd-verbatim.md`).

## Nature of the role
Product designer embedded with Product Engineers, PMs, PMMs and peer designers, taking ideas
idea→completion across Studio, docs, marketing and tooling. Explicitly a *systems* design role:
"creating product primitives that can be reused, extended, and combined in different contexts."

## Requirements

**Genuine strengths:**

**Unverified — must confirm before writing anything:**

  Designer resume is honest or fabricated.

## Strategic note
Applying to Frontend Engineer and Product Designer at the same company simultaneously is not
disqualifying at ~400 people, but the two applications should tell one coherent story: an engineer
with unusually strong product/design ownership. They should not read as two different people.
