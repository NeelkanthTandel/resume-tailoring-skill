# Tailoring report — Supabase Product Designer

## Verdict, stated plainly
This is a **portfolio-first pipeline** and the candidate has no portfolio. The resume below is
honest and well-positioned, but it cannot carry the application on its own. The Frontend Engineer
application is materially stronger and should be treated as the primary shot at Supabase.

## The honesty constraint that shaped this resume
Confirmed with the candidate 2026-08-28: **he did not do the design work on Gullynest.** The UI was
AI-generated from his product direction. His real design artifacts are **Tvisi** (Figma: flows,
screens, IA) and **college projects**.

Therefore this resume:
- **Does not** claim Figma work, mockups, or visual design on Gullynest.
- **Does** claim product ownership, user research, roadmap decisions and information architecture on
  Gullynest, all of which he genuinely did.
- **Leads the design credential with Tvisi**, moved above Neev so the one real end-to-end design
  story sits high on the page.
- Positions him as **Design Engineer**, per his choice — an engineer with product ownership, not a
  product designer with a thin portfolio. Defensible under questioning, and consistent with the
  Frontend application so the two read as one person.

## What would actually change the odds
One weekend, in priority order:
1. **A single written Gullynest case study.** Not screenshots — the reasoning. The three-supply-type
   problem is a genuinely interesting IA challenge: tenant handovers, direct owners and RERA agents
   are three different trust models that must coexist in one browsing experience. Frame it as
   problem → options considered → decision → what users did next. That one document answers D1, D3
   and D4 at once.
2. **A second case study on Tvisi**, since the Figma artifacts already exist. Two-sided marketplace,
   two audiences with opposite levels of digital literacy. Show the flows.
3. **Anything on D6.** If Gullynest has consistent components, document the pattern set — even a
   one-page inventory of primitives and where each is reused. It is the responsibility the JD
   describes most concretely and the one with zero evidence.

Without at least item 1, this application is very likely screened out regardless of resume quality.
